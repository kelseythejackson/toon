# toon cast

> **For maintainers and designers.** These are the brand files (character SVGs, logo, generator). Library users don't need them.

Four mascot characters in the **hybrid** style:
- a flat geometric body
- a hand-drawn ink outline drawn about 3px off-register (up-left of the fill)
- 2.4px round-capped ink strokes for faces and limbs

Every file here is generated by `build.py`. Change a parameter and run `python3 brand/build.py`; don't hand-edit the SVGs.

| | Shape | Personality | Signature detail | Brand color | Demo theme slot | Used in demos |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dot** | circle | curious, bouncy (the lead) | none: it's the logo | `#2F6BFF` | `--demo-primary` | Squash & stretch, Anticipation, Arcs, Appeal |
| **Brick** | rounded square | calm, unflappable (the heavy) | thick base line (weight) | `#E8442A` | `--demo-neutral` | Timing (heavy), Staging crowd |
| **Zip** | teardrop, hovers | excitable, quick (the light) | three speed-lines | `#FFB020` | `--demo-tertiary` | Timing (light), Follow through |
| **Loop** | tall capsule | theatrical (the show-off) | ink-line arms and feet | `#1F9E6E` | `--demo-secondary` | Pose to pose, Secondary action, Exaggeration, Staging hero |

Ink is `#1A1A1A`. The cast is genderless: use the names, never pronouns, in copy.

## Files
- `cast/<name>-<mood>.svg`: 200×200 and transparent, with the ground at y = 180. Moods:
  - `neutral`
  - `happy`: closed-arc eyes and an open smile
  - `surprised`: raised brows, an "o" mouth, and medium pupils (never tiny)
  - `focused`: a sideways glance, level brows and a tongue at the corner of the mouth
- `cast-lineup.svg`: all four on grid paper.
- `themed/cast-riso-light.svg`, `themed/cast-crayon-dark.svg`: the cast in two of the docs' site styles, using the demo token slots above.
- `logo/toon-mark-{48,32,16}.svg`: Dot as the mark. The 16px version drops the smile and enlarges the eyes.
- `cast-sheet.png`: a preview of everything.

## Rules that keep the cast friendly
These address what made the old characters feel creepy.
- **No gloss or 3D shading.** Flat fills only.
- **Every face has brows and a mouth.** Pupils are at least 46% of the eye's width.
- **No tails or fleshy shapes.** Motion is shown with ink speed-lines.
- **No skin-like pinks on bodies.** The only pink is the tongue in `focused`.
- **Facial features need at least 3:1 contrast against the body.** On dark bodies they switch to white (`on_body()` in `build.py`).
- **The ink outline needs at least 3:1 contrast against the background.** In dark mode it uses the theme's light ink.

## Checked contrast

| Palette | Faces on body | Outline vs background |
|---|---|---|
| Brand, on paper | 3.9–9.5:1 | 16.7:1 |
| Riso light | 3.9–16.8:1 | 16.5:1 |
| Crayon dark | 7.5–11.8:1 | 14.3:1 |

Zip's amber is pale against paper (1.8:1). The outline is what makes Zip identifiable, so never draw Zip without it.
