Even a 2-D animation should feel like it has depth (*solid drawing*: treating shapes as volumes with weight and light). Shading shows where the light is and that the shape is round. A contact shadow shows where the object sits in space and how high it is.

## Solid drawing in Canvas

`drawSolid()` paints a shaded sphere from one color and a light direction. `shade()` lightens or darkens a color, so the box's faces can follow the same light. `groundShadow()` shrinks and fades the shadow with height.

## Solid drawing: tuning tips

- **Keep one light source.** Every highlight in the scene sits on the same side, and every shadow on the other. The CSS preset always lights from the top left, so pass the same `light` to `drawSolid()` when you mix the two.
- **Anchor the shadow to the ground.** The shadow stays on the floor and changes size and opacity. It never rises with the object, or the object seems to float.
- **Push the shadow away from the light.** Offsetting it a few px to the side opposite the light, as the globe demo does, makes the light direction easier to read.
- **Keep volume constant.** Solid drawing and [squash and stretch](../squash-and-stretch/) work together: the shape deforms, but it never gains or loses mass.
