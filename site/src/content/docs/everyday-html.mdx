Each example below is a plain HTML element with one toon class on it, running on the screen. Press **Show code** to see the snippet; copy it into a page that loads `toon.css` and it works as shown. The demos only add page styling (colors, borders and fonts).

Under each screen, **Replay** plays a one-shot entrance again and **Slow motion** runs the example at quarter speed, so you can watch each pose. Drag the handle on the screen's right edge to try the example at a narrower width.

Every preset here switches off when the viewer has asked for reduced motion, and one-shot entrances jump to where they end.

## A button that squashes

To make a button feel physical, add `.toon-press`. It dips while it's held and springs a little past its size on release.

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='button.html'
  before={'<link rel="stylesheet" href="toon.css">\n'}
/>

At the default `--toon-squash` of .22 it dips to 91%. Add `.toon-subtle` to halve that. To make it wind up on hover as well, add `.toon-anticipate` from the card below.

## A card that winds up on hover

To show that a card is ready to open, add `.toon-anticipate`. On hover or keyboard focus it crouches, springs up and settles 4px higher.

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='hover-card.html'
  before={'<link rel="stylesheet" href="toon.css">\n'}
/>

The wind-up takes 560 ms, 70% of `--toon-duration`. Turn `--toon-exaggeration` up or down to change the crouch and the jump.

## A list that settles in

To bring a list in item by item, put `.toon-follow` on the list, not the items. Each item arrives 70 ms after the one above and swings past its stop before it settles (follow-through).

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='list.html'
  before={'<link rel="stylesheet" href="toon.css">\n'}
/>

Set the stagger with `--toon-overlap`. The first 12 items stagger on their own; set `--toon-i` inline on later ones.

## A dialog that pops in

To open a dialog, popover or tooltip with a friendly bounce, add `.toon-pop-in`. It grows from nothing, overshoots to 127% and wobbles to rest in 800 ms.

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='dialog.html'
  before={'<link rel="stylesheet" href="toon.css">\n'}
/>

To play a one-shot class again in your own page, remove the class, read `offsetWidth` and add the class back. [Replay a one-shot effect](../guides/frameworks/#replay-a-one-shot-effect-without-a-framework) shows the script, and how to do it in React, Vue and Svelte.

## A toast that settles

To slide one message in, add `.toon-settle`. It's the list's entrance for a single element: it stops, then its weight tips it back, past and level.

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='toast.html'

It starts half of `--toon-distance` to the left (120px by default).

## Tabs that put one in focus

To direct attention to one choice, put `.toon-stage` on the group. The hovered or focused child grows to 104% while the others shrink to 96% and fade to 75% opacity, still readable.

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='tabs.html'
  before={'<link rel="stylesheet" href="toon.css">\n'}
/>

Add `.is-featured` to the child that should stay in focus when nothing is hovered.

## Add to cart along an arc

To throw something into a cart, wrap it in `.toon-arc`. The wrapper moves sideways at a steady speed while its child rises and falls like a real throw (an arc).

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='add-to-cart.html'
  join
  before={'<link rel="stylesheet" href="toon.css">\n'}
/>

Set how far it travels with `--toon-distance` and how high it goes with `--toon-height`. The [throwing recipe](../recipes/#throwing-something-along-a-path) aims at a real target with `toon.js`.

## A send button that launches

To make a button take off when it's pressed, add `.toon-launch`. It pulls back, shoots forward `--toon-distance`, flies past and settles there.

## A bell that sways

To hint that something new is waiting, add `.toon-sway`. It rocks 4° each way from its base, one swing every 2.4 seconds.

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='bell.html'
  before={'<link rel="stylesheet" href="toon.css">\n'}
/>

A small, slow motion like this supports the page without competing with it (secondary action).

## A bouncing loader

To show that something is loading, bounce a ball with `.toon-bounce` and put `.toon-bounce-shadow` on its shadow. The ball squashes on the ground, stretches at speed and goes round at the top.

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='loader.html'
  before={'<link rel="stylesheet" href="toon.css">\n'}
/>

Lower `--toon-height` for a small loader (60px here), or add `.toon-light` for a quick, squashy bounce.

## An avatar that breathes and blinks

To make a mascot or empty state feel alive, add `.toon-breathe` to the body and `.toon-blink` to each eye. It breathes every 2 seconds and blinks every 4 seconds.

<Recipe
  dir='html'
  file='avatar.html'
  before={'<link rel="stylesheet" href="toon.css">\n'}
/>

Both clocks follow `--toon-duration`, so one property slows the whole character down.
