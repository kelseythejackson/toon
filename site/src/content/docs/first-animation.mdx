This page assumes toon is on your page; see [Install toon](../install/). `toon.css` animates page elements with classes, and `toon.js` animates on `<canvas>`. Use either one, or both.

## Your first CSS animation

With `toon.css` included, two classes make a bouncing ball. A second element under it makes its contact shadow (the shadow on the ground, which shrinks as the ball rises). The ball jumps 140px, so the box around it leaves that much room above:

<Recipe dir="start" file="first-css.html" show={[0]} demo={false} />

Tune it with custom properties on the element, or on `:root` to change everything at once. Here the ball gets more squash (it flattens further when it lands), a higher bounce and a slower tempo. `--toon-tempo` multiplies every duration, so a higher tempo is *slower*:

<Recipe dir="start" file="first-css.html" show={[1]} />

## Your first Canvas animation

With `toon.js` included, this throws a ball back and forth along an [arc](../principles/arcs/) (the curved path a thrown thing follows). Each frame works out the ball's speed and direction, so it stretches the way it's moving:
