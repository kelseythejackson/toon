04 · Straight ahead vs pose to pose

Straight ahead

Smoke drawn frame after frame at 24 fps: each frame grows out of the previous one. Spontaneous, fluid, hard to plan.

Pose to pose

Loop's wave, built from 5 key poses (ghosted). poses() draws every in-between. Planned, precise, easy to retime.

CSS flipbook: .toon-sprite

Eight flame frames drawn straight ahead (each one perturbs the last) into a sprite sheet, then played with steps() so there are no in-betweens.
