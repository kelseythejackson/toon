Squash & stretch

Dot flattens on contact and stretches along its velocity, keeping its volume: as it gets wider it gets shorter. Also on show: timing (gravity sets the beat), slow in and slow out (the trail dots bunch up at the top of each bounce) and arcs (the trail is a curve). The CSS version is in Squash & stretch in CSS.

Try it Drag Exaggeration to 0 for a rigid ball, or toward 2 for rubber.
